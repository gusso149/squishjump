Squish Jump Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Squish Jump ("the Game") handles your personal information.

Information We Collect

Squish Jump does not collect, store, or process any personal data. The Game does not use any databases, local storage, or server-side storage of any kind.

Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services, which may collect information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for game progression (achievements, leaderboards, and sign-in). Data handling is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: The Game displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad serving and personalization. The Game is configured to comply with Google Play's Families Policy and uses age-appropriate ad content. Data handling is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

Squish Jump does not store any user data. No databases, files, or other storage mechanisms are used by the Game itself. Any data related to game progression is managed entirely by Google Play Services.

Data Deletion

Since Squish Jump does not collect or store any user data, there is no data to delete. For data managed by Google Play Services or Google AdMob, please refer to your Google Account settings to manage or delete that data.

Children's Privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
