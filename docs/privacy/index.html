Untitled Success Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Untitled Success ("the Game") handles your personal information.

Information We Collect

Untitled Success does not collect, store, or process any personal data. The Game does not use any databases, local storage, or server-side storage of any kind.

Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services, which may collect information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for game progression (achievements, leaderboards, and sign-in). Data handling is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising: The Game displays ads provided by third-party ad networks. These networks may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. Please refer to the respective ad provider's privacy policy for details.

Data Storage

Untitled Success does not store any user data. No databases, files, or other storage mechanisms are used by the Game itself. Any data related to game progression is managed entirely by Google Play Services.

Children's Privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
